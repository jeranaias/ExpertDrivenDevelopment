EDD Framework Reminders

Centaur: distinct phases. Human designs. AI builds. Human verifies. Human refines. Human accepts. Best for strict business rules.

Cyborg: continuous back-and-forth. Discover requirements while building. Best for exploratory work where the shape is not yet clear.

Tool choice: genai.mil and Ask Sage are both first-class. Pick per task. Note which one handled the work and why. Switch when one stalls.

Frontier Recognition: name the specific task types where AI is reliable and where it is not. Capture failures in real time. Your frontier map gets sharper every cycle.

Capability Gap Map: name the wall. Specific connector. Specific license. Specific permission. Beneficiary count and time savings. That row becomes policy evidence.
